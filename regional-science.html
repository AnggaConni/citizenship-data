Meteorological context

NASA POWER
Monthly context for temperature, precipitation and relative humidity.
World Bank water indicators are country-level statistics, not site measurements. NASA POWER values are gridded meteorological estimates—not discharge or water availability. Check indicator year, source notes, and local hydrological observations before scientific or policy use.

Data sources and methods
Biosphere reserves: UNESCO MAB dataset (mab001) · Global geoparks: UNESCO Global Geoparks (eg0001) · Marine indicators: Marine Biodiversity by Ocean Area (ioc001) · Marine occurrences: OBIS · Climate: NASA POWER · Publications: OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Crossref and OpenAIRE Graph · Institutions: ROR.
How to interpret recommendations
Results are selected by semantic similarity to a user-written question. The tool does not generate a scientific consensus, assess all methodological quality, or guarantee that an article is open access. Some metadata or links may be missing.
Independent exploratory prototype; not an official institutional product or endorsement. Dataset records, photographs, article metadata and map tiles retain their own licences and attribution requirements. The marine indicator source contains fields derived from other datasets; this page intentionally omits IUCN-threatened-species fields while that source component's provenance and reuse requirements are not separately assessed.
